## Capacity statement

The application has no fixed work-image slot limit: the image array and normalized `work_images` rows grow with every accepted upload. Practical limits still come from the client's browser/device memory, the 10 MB per-file check, request duration, and the Cloudinary account quota. It should not be described as literally infinite storage.
